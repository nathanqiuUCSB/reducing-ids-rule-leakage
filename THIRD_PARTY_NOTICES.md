## Emerging Threats Open Rules

This repository redistributes a small set of Suricata detection rules derived
from the Emerging Threats (ET) Open ruleset. All included SIDs fall within the
2,000,000–2,799,999 ET Open range.

ET-derived rule text appears in:

- `fixtures/dataset/*.json`
- `experiments/single-clue-targeted-v1/manifest.json`

Official ET Open distribution:
https://rules.emergingthreats.net/open/suricata/

`benign_sources/` contains download metadata and a checksummed downloader for
third-party benign PCAPs. Those binaries are not redistributed here. Review each
source's terms before downloading.
